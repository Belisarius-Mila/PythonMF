Our Life with AnimalsFIRST SCREENKate calls Lucy.Kate: “Hi, Lucy. Do you want to go to the cinema today?”Lucy: “Hi, Kate. Yes, I do. What film do you want?”Kate: “A film about animals. The film is Our Life with Animals.”Lucy: “Great! I love animal films. I call Peter and Tom.”Lucy calls Peter.Lucy: “Hi, Peter. We go to the cinema. Do you want to go with us?”Peter: “Hi, Lucy. Yes, I want to go. I like animals. What time do we meet?”Lucy: “We meet at four o’clock, near the park.”Peter: “Good. I call Tom.”Peter calls Tom.Peter: “Hi, Tom. We meet Kate and Lucy. We go to the cinema.”Tom: “Hi, Peter! Yes, I come. I love animals in the zoo.”Peter: “Me too. We walk in the park first.”Tom: “Perfect!”SECOND SCREENThey meet near the park.Kate: “Hi everyone!”Lucy, Peter, Tom: “Hi!”They start to walk through the park. The day is nice. Lucy sees a dog.
Lucy: “Look! A dog. It is very big.”They hear birds in the trees.Tom: “I hear birds.”Peter: “Yes. Birds sing. I like it.”Lucy: “They are nice. The colors! Blue, green, yellow, white...”THIRD SCREEN They walk to a small pond.Lucy: “Look, ducks!”Kate: “Two small ducks and one big duck.”Tom: “And look—fish in the water!”Peter: “It is red, Tom. It is very nice”.Now they see a horse in a field.Kate: “Is that a horse?”Lucy: “Yes, a horse. It is grey and black.”Tom: “I like horses. And I like cows too.”Peter: “And I like sheep and goats.”FOURTH SCREENAt the cinema they buy tickets.Peter: “Four tickets, please.”Lucy: “And we buy popcorn.”Tom: “I buy a drink.”Lucy: “Look, a lion!”Peter: “And look—a giraffe. It is very tall.”Kate: “I like the zebra. Black and white.Lucy: “The Elephants are grey and big.”Peter: “Yes, and very smart.”Tom: “I like the baby elephant.”FIFTH SCREENKate: “Monkeys are funny.”Tom: “They jump and run!”Lucy: “The crocodile is dangerous.”Kate: “Yes, but the hippo is dangerous too.”Tom: “The hippo is big, but it looks slow.”Peter: “In water it is fast.”SIXTH SCREENKate: “The film is really good.”Lucy: “Yes. I love the lion and the elephant.”Tom: “I love the giraffe and the monkey.”Peter: “I love the zebra and the crocodile.”Kate: “Now I want to go to the zoo.”Lucy: “Me too! We go another day.”Tom: “Yes! We see lions, elephants, giraffes, zebras and monkeys…”Peter: “And maybe we see rhinos too.”They smile. All say bye. They go home.
