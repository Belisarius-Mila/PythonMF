# Scene 3 - Field Path by the Village

Cesky nazev: Cesta mezi poli kolem vesnice
Vyukovy cil: osoby `I, you, he, she, it, we, they` pres konkretni postavy
Navaznost: skupina vyjde z lesa na pole a vidi v dalky vesnici

## Popis sceny cesky

Zviratka opusti les a jdou cestou mezi poli. V dalky je mala vesnice, plot,
stodola nebo vetrny mlyn. Sunny beha po plote, Fiona ukazuje spravny smer,
Bruno nese lucernu i ve dne, Benji drzi mapu a Bunny se drzi pri nem.

Scena je dobra pro osoby, protoze postavy jsou rozmistene v prostoru.

## Obrazova varianta A

Siroka polni cesta, zviratka jdou v rade zleva doprava. Kazda postava ma vlastni
jasne misto.

## Obrazova varianta B

Rozcesti u plotu. Fiona ukazuje doprava, Benji kontroluje mapu a ostatni cekaji.

## Obrazova varianta C

Sunny je na plote, Bruno u branky, Benji a Bunny na ceste, Fiona u sipky z vetvi.
Dete klika na postavy podle `he`, `she`, `we`, `they`.

## Interakce pro Matyska

- UI napoveda rekne: `He is Bruno.` Matysek klikne na Bruna.
- UI napoveda rekne: `She is Fiona.` Matysek klikne na Fionu.
- UI napoveda rekne: `They are Benji and Bunny.` Jemne pulzuji dve postavy.
- `It is a map.` Matysek klikne na mapu.

## Dialog A - pronouns pres hlavni petici

```text
Fiona: He is Bruno.
Bruno: I am Bruno.
Bruno: She is Fiona.
Fiona: I am Fiona.
Benji: It is a map.
Benji: I have a map.
Sunny: They are friends.
Bunny: We are friends.
```

## Dialog B - cesta a smer

```text
Benji: I am here.
Bunny: You are here.
Fiona: He is Bruno.
Bruno: She is Fiona.
Sunny: I am on the fence!
Fiona: It is a village.
All: We are going to the lake.
```

## Dialog C - vlastnosti

```text
Fiona: I am clever.
Sunny: I am fast.
Bruno: I am strong.
Bunny: I am small.
Benji: You are my friends.
Fiona: They are ready.
```

## Proc se scena hodi

Pole a cesta daji vizualne cisty prostor. Dite vidi jednotlive postavy oddelene,
takze `he/she/they` lze ucit bez textove tabulky.
