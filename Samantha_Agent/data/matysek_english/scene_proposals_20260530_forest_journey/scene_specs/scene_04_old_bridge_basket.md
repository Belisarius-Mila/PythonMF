# Scene 4 - Old Bridge and Basket

Cesky nazev: Stary mostek a ztraceny kosik
Vyukovy cil: `I have`, jemne `you have`, `he has`, `she has`, `we have`
Navaznost: zviratka u potucku najdou kosik a porovnavaji, kdo co ma

## Popis sceny cesky

Na kraji lesa je maly dreveny mostek pres potucek. Vedle mostku lezi kosik s
jablky a drobnostmi. Kazda postava ma nejaky predmet: Benji mapu, Bunny mrkev,
Bruno lucernu, Fiona salu, Sunny orisky. Scena se primo hodi pro `have`.

## Obrazova varianta A

Kosik u mostku, predmety polozene kolem. Zviratka stoji vedle svych predmetu.

## Obrazova varianta B

Predmety jsou v kosiku a Matysek je "rozdava" kliknutim. Benji dostane mapu,
Bunny mrkev, Bruno lucernu, Fiona salu, Sunny orisky.

## Obrazova varianta C

Mostek je zavreny pomyslnou branou. Otevre se, kdyz Matysek pomuze vsem rict,
co maji.

## Interakce pro Matyska

- Postava rekne `I have a carrot.` Matysek klikne na mrkev.
- UI napoveda rekne `Who has a lantern?` Matysek klikne na Bruna nebo lucernu.
- Po trech spravnych kliknutich se mostek rozzari.

## Dialog A - jen `I have`

```text
Benji: I have a map.
Bunny: I have a carrot.
Bruno: I have a lantern.
Fiona: I have a scarf.
Sunny: I have nuts.
Fiona: Good. We have what we need.
```

## Dialog B - `have/has` jemne

```text
Fiona: Benji has a map.
Benji: I have a map.
Bruno: Bunny has a carrot.
Bunny: I have a carrot.
Benji: Bruno has a lantern.
Bruno: I have a lantern.
Fiona: We have a basket.
```

## Dialog C - hledani predmetu

```text
Fiona: Where is my scarf?
Sunny: I have nuts, not a scarf.
Benji: I have a map.
Bunny: I have a carrot.
Bruno: She has a scarf!
Fiona: Yes. I have my scarf.
```

## Proc se scena hodi

`Have` je nejlepsi ucit pres predmety v ruce. Mostek dava jasny dramaticky cil:
kdyz vime, co kdo ma, muzeme jit dal.
