# English dialogue pack

Tento soubor obsahuje jen anglicke dialogy bez ceskych vysvetleni. Slouzi pro
rychly poslechovy a scenaristicky review.

## Scene 1 - Clearing Meeting

### A - simplest

```text
Benji: Hello! I am Benji.
Bunny: Hi! I am Bunny.
Bruno: Hello. I am Bruno.
Fiona: Hi. I am Fiona.
Sunny: Hello! I am Sunny.
Owl: They are friends.
All: We are friends!
```

### B - how are you

```text
Benji: Hello, Bunny.
Bunny: Hello, Benji.
Benji: How are you?
Bunny: I am happy.
Bruno: Hello. I am Bruno.
Fiona: Hi. I am Fiona.
Sunny: I am Sunny!
Owl: Benji is a dog. Bunny is a rabbit. Fiona is a fox.
```

### C - more social

```text
Bunny: Hi, Benji. You are here!
Benji: Yes. I am here.
Bruno: Good morning. I am Bruno.
Fiona: Hello, Bruno. I am Fiona.
Sunny: And I am Sunny!
Benji: We are going to the lake.
Bunny: We are going together.
```

## Scene 2 - Mossy Stump Rest

### A - feelings

```text
Bunny: I am tired.
Benji: I am tired too.
Sunny: I am not tired!
Fiona: You are fast, Sunny.
Bruno: We are okay.
Owl: We are friends.
```

### B - how are you

```text
Benji: Bruno, how are you?
Bruno: I am fine.
Bunny: Fiona, how are you?
Fiona: I am happy.
Sunny: I am hungry!
Bunny: You are funny, Sunny.
```

### C - to be drill in story

```text
Owl: Listen.
Benji: I am Benji. I am ready.
Bunny: I am Bunny. I am tired.
Bruno: I am Bruno. I am strong.
Fiona: I am Fiona. I am clever.
Sunny: I am Sunny. I am little.
All: We are ready.
```

## Scene 3 - Field Path by the Village

### A - pronouns

```text
Owl: He is Bruno.
Bruno: I am Bruno.
Owl: She is Fiona.
Fiona: I am Fiona.
Owl: It is a map.
Benji: I have a map.
Owl: They are friends.
Bunny: We are friends.
```

### B - path and direction

```text
Benji: I am here.
Bunny: You are here.
Fiona: He is Bruno.
Bruno: She is Fiona.
Sunny: I am on the fence!
Owl: It is a village.
All: We are going to the lake.
```

### C - character traits

```text
Fiona: I am clever.
Sunny: I am fast.
Bruno: I am strong.
Bunny: I am small.
Benji: You are my friends.
Owl: They are ready.
```

## Scene 4 - Old Bridge and Basket

### A - I have

```text
Benji: I have a map.
Bunny: I have a carrot.
Bruno: I have a lantern.
Fiona: I have a scarf.
Sunny: I have nuts.
Owl: Good. We have what we need.
```

### B - have and has

```text
Owl: Benji has a map.
Benji: I have a map.
Owl: Bunny has a carrot.
Bunny: I have a carrot.
Owl: Bruno has a lantern.
Bruno: I have a lantern.
Fiona: We have a basket.
```

### C - object search

```text
Fiona: Where is my scarf?
Sunny: I have nuts, not a scarf.
Benji: I have a map.
Bunny: I have a carrot.
Bruno: She has a scarf!
Fiona: Yes. I have my scarf.
```

## Scene 5 - Second Forest Gate

### A - identity questions

```text
Owl: Are you Benji?
Benji: Yes, I am.
Owl: Are you Bunny?
Benji: No, I am not. I am Benji.
Bunny: I am Bunny.
Owl: Good. You are ready.
```

### B - group check

```text
Owl: Are you ready?
All: Yes, we are.
Owl: Is he Bruno?
Bunny: Yes, he is.
Owl: Is she Fiona?
Benji: Yes, she is.
Sunny: Are we at the lake?
Fiona: Not yet!
```

### C - comic mix-up

```text
Sunny: I am Benji!
Benji: No, you are Sunny.
Sunny: I am Sunny!
Fiona: She is funny.
Bruno: We are tired.
Bunny: We are almost there.
```

```text
Benji: We are here!
Bunny: We are happy.
Bruno: We are friends.
Fiona: They are here.
Sunny: We have a feast!
Owl: Well done.
```

### B - object recap

```text
Benji: I have a map.

Bruno: I have a lantern.
```
